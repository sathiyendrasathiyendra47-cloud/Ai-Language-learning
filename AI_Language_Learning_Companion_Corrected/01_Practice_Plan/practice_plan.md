# 14-Day English Practice Plan (A1)
Daily practice: 20–30 minutes.

| Day | Topic | Activity | Evidence |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Write five self-introduction sentences and take the quiz before practice | Day 1 scores |
| 2 | Greetings | Practise greetings and polite expressions | Chat transcript |
| 3 | Vocabulary | Learn ten everyday words and make example sentences | Vocabulary notes |
| 4 | Grammar | Practise am, is, and are | Five sentences |
| 5 | Daily routine | Describe your routine using simple present | Corrected paragraph |
| 6 | Questions | Practise who, what, where, when, and why | Eight Q&As |
| 7 | Review | Review vocabulary and grammar | Midpoint scores |
| 8 | Shopping | Role-play a shop conversation | Transcript |
| 9 | Food | Practise ordering food politely | Transcript |
| 10 | Directions | Ask for and give directions | Dialogue |
| 11 | Grammar correction | Test the same ten sentences in three AI tools | Mistake log |
| 12 | Pronunciation | Compare AI speech with dictionary audio or a fluent speaker | Audio and notes |
| 13 | Free conversation | Have a five-minute beginner conversation | Transcript and reflection |
| 14 | Final assessment | Repeat a comparable test and compare it with Day 1 | Day 14 scores |

## Daily routine
1. Review vocabulary for five minutes.
2. Practise the day's topic with AI for 10–15 minutes.
3. Check and record mistakes for five minutes.
4. Speak aloud for 2–5 minutes.

Record measured scores; do not estimate or invent improvement.
