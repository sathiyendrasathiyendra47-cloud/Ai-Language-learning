# Illustrated Vocabulary Poster Guide
## Title
**Learn 10 Everyday English Words — English and Tamil**

## Vocabulary
- Hello — வணக்கம்
- Water — தண்ணீர்
- Food — உணவு
- Friend — நண்பர்
- School — பள்ளி
- Book — புத்தகம்
- House — வீடு
- Happy — மகிழ்ச்சியாக (example: “I am happy.”)
- Learn — கற்றுக்கொள்
- Thank you — நன்றி

Use Canva or another free design tool. Include the English word, Tamil meaning, a simple illustration, and a short example sentence for each entry.

## Quality check
Proofread every Tamil label, ensure illustrations match the words, use readable fonts and good contrast, and add text manually in the design editor rather than trusting text rendered inside AI images. Save the finished poster as PNG, JPG, or PDF in this folder.
