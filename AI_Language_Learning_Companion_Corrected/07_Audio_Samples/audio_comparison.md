## Procedure
1. Generate or play each sentence using an available free/free-tier text-to-speech service, such as ElevenLabs, or a translation service with audio such as Google Translate.
2. Find the same words or sentence in a reputable learner's dictionary with audio, or ask a fluent/native speaker to record it with consent.
3. Save audio in this folder if the service permits download and reuse.
4. Compare consonants, vowels, word stress, pauses, clarity, and naturalness.
5. Record accent differences without assuming every difference is an error.
