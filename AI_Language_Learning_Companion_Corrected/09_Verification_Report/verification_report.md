# Verification Report Template

## Objective
Evaluate grammar correction and English–Tamil translation quality from ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude using the same 10 sentences.

## Method
1. Use identical sentences and prompts in all three tools.
2. Save actual outputs or screenshots.
3. Check grammar against a reputable grammar reference.
4. Check Tamil translations with a reliable dictionary or fluent Tamil speaker.
5. Accept valid alternatives when meaning and context are preserved.

## Accuracy
Grammar handling accuracy = (number of sentences handled acceptably ÷ number of sentences evaluated) × 100.

Report the denominator and scoring rules. Consider reporting ambiguous sentence 2 separately. Sentence 7 is acceptable in ordinary conversation. Evaluate translation quality separately from grammar correction.

## Limitations
A 10-sentence test is small and cannot represent every learner, dialect, accent, or conversation context. Report actual findings only; do not fabricate scores or claim external verification that was not performed.
