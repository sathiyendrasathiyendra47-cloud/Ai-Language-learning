# Responsible AI: Limits and Safety

## Cultural nuance
Literal translations may miss politeness, idioms, social context, or relationships between speakers. Ask for context and verify with a fluent speaker when possible.

## Dialect and accent variation
English and Tamil have regional varieties. A phrase may be valid in one dialect or informal setting even if a tool prefers another form. Identify variety and register before calling a response an error.

## Over-trust
AI can confidently provide incorrect corrections, explanations, or translations. Compare outputs with reliable references and record uncertainty.

## Privacy
Do not enter passwords, private identifiers, confidential information, or unnecessary personal data. Review each service's data and privacy settings.

## Fair evaluation
- Use identical prompts and sentences for all tools.
- Separate grammar accuracy from translation quality.
- Accept multiple correct translations where meaning and context are preserved.
- Save evidence of outputs and verification.
- Do not invent findings, accuracy scores, audio, or completed activities.

## Conclusion template
“During this small test, [tool] handled [observed strengths] and made [observed errors]. This suggests that free AI tools can support low-stakes beginner practice when learners verify feedback, but they should not be treated as infallible teachers or authorities on every dialect and cultural context.”
