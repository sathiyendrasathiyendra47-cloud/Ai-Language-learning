# A1 English Quiz — 10 Questions
Choose one answer per question. The answer key is at the end.

1. Choose the correct sentence. A. She go to school. B. She goes to school. C. She going to school.
2. Fill in the blank: I ___ a student. A. am B. is C. are
3. Choose the correct article: ___ orange. A. A B. An C. The an
4. What is the simple past of “go”? A. Goed B. Going C. Went
5. Choose the correct sentence. A. They are happy. B. They is happy. C. They am happy.
6. Common Tamil meaning of “water”? A. உணவு B. தண்ணீர் C. புத்தகம்
7. Complete: He doesn't ___ coffee. A. likes B. like C. liking
8. Choose the standard direct question. A. Where you are going? B. Where are you going? C. Where going you are?
9. Tamil meaning of “Thank you”? A. நன்றி B. வணக்கம் C. மன்னிக்கவும்
10. Choose the correct sentence. A. I can to swim. B. I can swimming. C. I can swim.

## Answer key and explanations
1. B — Third-person singular simple present takes -s/-es.
2. A — Use “am” with “I.”
3. B — “Orange” begins with a vowel sound, so use “an.”
4. C — “Went” is the simple past of “go.”
5. A — Use “are” with “they.”
6. B — தண்ணீர் is a common Tamil word for water.
7. B — “Doesn't” is followed by the base verb.
8. B — In a standard direct question, “are” comes before the subject.
9. A — நன்றி means “thank you” or “thanks.”
10. C — A modal such as “can” is followed by the base verb without “to.”
