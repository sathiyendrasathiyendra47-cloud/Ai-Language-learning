# Common Test: 10 English Sentences
Use these exact sentences and the same evaluation prompt with ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude.

| No. | Test sentence | Reference decision |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | He go to school every day. | Incorrect in standard English: “He goes to school every day.” |
| 2 | I am go to college. | Incorrect as written. Depending on intended meaning: “I am going to college” or “I go to college.” Record the tool's interpretation. |
| 3 | She don't like tea. | Incorrect in standard English: “She doesn't like tea.” |
| 4 | They is my friends. | Incorrect: “They are my friends.” |
| 5 | I have a apple. | Incorrect: “I have an apple.” |
| 6 | Yesterday I go to market. | For a completed past event: “Yesterday I went to the market.” |
| 7 | He is taller than me. | Acceptable in ordinary contemporary English. “He is taller than I am” is also possible and more formal. |
| 8 | I didn't went there. | Incorrect: “I didn't go there.” |
| 9 | Where you are going? | For a standard direct question: “Where are you going?” Context can affect interpretation. |
| 10 | I can to swim. | Incorrect: “I can swim.” |

## Evaluation prompt
For each numbered sentence, assess grammar and meaning. If incorrect, provide the smallest correction that preserves likely intended meaning. Translate the corrected sentence into Tamil and explain the rule in one sentence. If acceptable in ordinary conversation, label it acceptable. If ambiguous, state the ambiguity.

## Recording rules
Record actual tool outputs, keep screenshots or copied responses, and verify translations independently. Do not treat every wording difference as an error. Sentence 2 depends on intended meaning; sentence 7 is acceptable in ordinary conversation.
